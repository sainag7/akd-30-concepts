AKD 30.0 website concepts

Three directions for the First in Flight season. Each one is a full homepage with the real date, venue, partners and visitor info. Open one and scroll. The motion is where each concept comes alive.

Flight Deck

Top Gun energy. The page is a fighter cockpit HUD. Scrolling climbs you from the runway to 30,000 ft, so you hit FL300 right as the page ends.

Signature
Altitude, speed and heading tapes driven by scroll. Headings get locked on as they arrive. The lineup is a radar scope.
Type
Saira, from condensed to extra wide
Palette
Open Flight Deck

Kitty Hawk 1903

The literal First in Flight. The site is the Wright brothers' patent blueprint, and the event details are the drafting notes on the Flyer.

Signature
The Flyer draws itself on load. A scroll-driven flight log retraces the four 1903 flights and then AKD's. Team photos are printed as cyanotypes.
Type
Old Standard TT with Architects Daughter for drafting notes
Palette
Open Kitty Hawk

Carolina Skies

A premium airline in Carolina blue. You start in a window seat. Scroll and the window opens into the whole sky, and the show is cleared for takeoff.

Signature
The cabin window expands to full screen. The lineup is a split-flap departures board. The board is a seat map, and the page ends on a runway.
Type
Archivo, expanded for livery-style headlines
Palette
Open Carolina Skies
