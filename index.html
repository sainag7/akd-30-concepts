Round 4 Lineup Poster layout in the Air Mail style

Vintage Poster

The Lineup Poster's pages and layout, printed on Air Mail's aged paper. Sepia photos, Rye wood type, stamps, a postmark, a compass rose and air mail stripes.

Signature
A pencil-sketch Wright Flyer flies off as you scroll, and teams pop up as postcards. The telegram, split-flap board and postmark countdown are all included.
Type
Rye wood type with Special Elite
Palette

Round 3 Vintage, after the "Applications are live" post

Air Mail

Old paper, sepia ink and 1920s aviation ephemera, matching the applications post. Wood-type headlines, a pencil-sketch biplane, a compass rose and air mail stamps.

Signature
A little plane flies a dashed route down the whole page as you scroll. The application details type themselves out on a telegram, and teams are sepia postcards.
Type
Rye wood type with Special Elite

Round 2 Dance-first, inspired by real comp and festival sites

Air Show

Daylight air show energy. Team photos fly in formation across a Carolina-blue sky, trailing smoke, behind giant poster type.

Signature
Smoke trails draw in behind the photo formation, which keeps climbing as you scroll. Teams scroll sideways in a photo strip.
Type
Big Shoulders Display with Hanken Grotesk
Palette

Round 1 First set of directions

Flight Deck

Top Gun energy. The page is a fighter cockpit HUD. Scrolling climbs you from the runway to 30,000 ft, so you hit FL300 right as the page ends.

Signature
Altitude, speed and heading tapes driven by scroll. Headings get locked on as they arrive. Teams show on a split-flap departures board.
Type
Saira, from condensed to extra wide
Palette
Open Flight Deck

Kitty Hawk 1903

The literal First in Flight. The site is the Wright brothers' patent blueprint, and the event details are the drafting notes on the Flyer.

Signature
The Flyer draws itself on load. A scroll-driven flight log retraces the four 1903 flights and then AKD's. Team photos are printed as cyanotypes.
Type
Old Standard TT with Architects Daughter for drafting notes
Palette
Open Kitty Hawk

Carolina Skies

A premium airline in Carolina blue. You start in a window seat. Scroll and the window opens into the whole sky, and the show is cleared for takeoff.

Signature
The cabin window expands to full screen. The lineup is a split-flap departures board. The board is a seat map, and the page ends on a runway.
Type
Archivo, expanded for livery-style headlines
Palette
Open Carolina Skies
