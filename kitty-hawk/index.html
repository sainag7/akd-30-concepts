Specification

Sheet 1 of 5

Be it known that the students of UNC Chapel Hill have invented a new and useful night of South Asian dance, of which the following is a specification.

What we claim is:

  1. A collegiate dance competition held on February 20, 2027, at the John H. Gattis–Wendell Tabb Theater.
  2. The competition of claim 1, comprising a Fusion division and a Bhangra division.
  3. The competition of claim 2, recognized as an official Legends partner competition.
  4. The competition of claim 1, further comprising you, in the audience.
